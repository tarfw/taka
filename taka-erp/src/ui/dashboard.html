TAKA Scientific — Operations Guide

Commercial Lifecycle Overview

End-to-end commercial operations across Abu Dhabi & Dubai facilities:

  1. Commercial Quotation: Draft customer proposal with item selection from 140 instruments and 5% UAE VAT.
  2. Sales Tax Invoice: Convert accepted quote to official FTA Tax Invoice, debiting AR (1200) and crediting Sales (4000) & Output VAT (2200).
  3. Supplier Procurement: Issue Purchase Order to Hanil, IKA, or Hach. Convert received goods to Vendor Bills with AP (2000).
  4. Payment Settlement: Receive customer bank wire into WIO Bank PJSC (1000) and credit AR (1200).
  5. FTA VAT 201 Return: Compile Box 1 standard supplies and Box 9 recoverable expenses.
